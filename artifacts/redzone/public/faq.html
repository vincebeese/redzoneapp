The Basics

What is the RZS AI Coach?
It's a sales coaching tool built on the Red Zone Selling system. You describe a real situation — a deal, a skill gap, a mindset challenge — and it coaches you the way Vince would. Specific, direct, and immediately actionable.
What are the three modes?
Deal Mode
Coaching on a specific active deal. Diagnoses the zone, names the play, gives you language to use.
Coach Mode
On-demand advice for any live selling situation. No deal required. Think of it as Vince on speed dial.
Mindset Mode
The mental game. Use it when you're pressing, processing a loss, or preparing for a high-stakes moment.
How do I know which mode to use?
Got a specific deal you're stuck on? Deal Mode. Facing a situation and not sure what play to run? Coach Mode. In your head and need to reset before your next call? Mindset Mode.

Seller Profile

What is the Seller Profile and do I need to fill it out?
It's five fields — your ICP, average deal size, sales cycle, win themes, and loss patterns. You don't have to fill it out, but every field you add makes the coaching sharper. Without it, the AI coaches cold. With it, it flags your known loss patterns before you repeat them.
Where do I find it?
Account → Seller Profile. Takes about two minutes.
What if I only fill out some of the fields?
No problem. The AI uses whatever is there and will ask for missing fields one at a time — one question per session, never more.

Deal Mode

What do I need to have ready before I start?
Just a deal you're actively working. Company name, where you think it stands, and a quick description of where you're stuck. The more specific you are, the sharper the coaching. It loves reading call transcripts — feed them if you have them.
What's a zone and which one should I pick?
Every deal lives in one of three zones — Yellow (qualification), Green (momentum), or Red (closing). Pick the one that matches where your deal actually is. If you're not sure, start with Yellow. You can also move your deal forward or back manually once you reach certain thresholds.
Why is it asking me to justify my zone selection?
If you start a deal in Green or Red Zone, the AI asks what forward signals put it there. That's not a test — it's the first coaching move. Zone accuracy matters. A deal classified in the wrong zone gets the wrong plays.
Zone accuracy is everything in the Red Zone Selling system. The AI challenges your classification to make sure the coaching that follows actually fits where you are.

Coach Mode

How is Coach Mode different from Deal Mode?
Deal Mode runs a structured diagnosis on one specific deal. Coach Mode is a direct conversation — you describe any selling situation and get coaching on it. No deal creation required.
Can I use Coach Mode for skill building?
Yes. Tell it what skill you want to sharpen — discovery, multi-threading, closing, negotiation — and it will drill you on it, including role-play if you want it.

Mindset Mode

What kind of situations is Mindset Mode for?
Bad quarter. Lost deal. Pressing on calls. Fear before a big presentation. Confidence dip. Any moment where the mental game is getting in the way of the selling game.
Is this therapy?
Ha, no. It's a performance coach. It acknowledges briefly, reframes fast, and gets you back to work. If what you're experiencing goes beyond a tough quarter, it will tell you and point you toward the right support.
