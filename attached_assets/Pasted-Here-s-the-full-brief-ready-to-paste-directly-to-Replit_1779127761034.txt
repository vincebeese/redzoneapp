# Replit Dev Brief — Web Search Research Feature
# Red Zone Selling AI Coach — Deal Mode & Coach Mode

---

## OVERVIEW

Add web search capability to Deal Mode and Coach Mode.
When a company name or person's name is introduced in
either mode, the coach proactively offers to research
them before coaching begins. The user controls whether
research runs — the coach never executes it automatically.

This is one feature with two query types:
- Company research
- Person research

Both use the same web search tool. Build them together.

---

## WHERE IT LIVES

Deal Mode — yes
Coach Mode — yes
Mindset Mode — no

---

## THE TRIGGER

When a company name or person's name is introduced in
a Deal Mode or Coach Mode conversation, the coach
pauses before coaching and makes one offer:

"Want me to research [Company] and/or [Person's Name]
before we dig in? I can pull company background, ICP
fit, and relevant intel on [Name] to sharpen your
approach."

One offer. One question. Not two separate prompts.

---

## USER RESPONSE OPTIONS

The user responds with one of four answers:

- Both → run company research first, person research
  second, then proceed to coaching
- Company only → run company research, skip person,
  then proceed to coaching
- Person only → run person research, skip company,
  then proceed to coaching
- No → skip research entirely, proceed directly
  to coaching

---

## COMPANY RESEARCH OUTPUT FORMAT

When company research is requested, run a web search
on the company name and return results in this exact
format:

---

COMPANY: [Name]
WHAT THEY DO: [one sentence description]
SIZE / STAGE: [headcount, funding stage, revenue
              if available]
RECENT NEWS: [anything relevant — funding rounds,
             leadership changes, product launches,
             layoffs, acquisitions, or anything that
             could affect the sale]

ICP FIT ASSESSMENT:
Based on your ICP — [pull stored ICP from user
profile] — this account [fits / partially fits /
falls outside] your ICP profile.

Here's why: [one to two sentences specific to their
stored ICP criteria]

[If fit] Green light on ICP. Let's qualify the rest.
[If partial] Possible fit but [specific gap].
Worth a conversation but go in eyes open.
[If outside] This account falls outside your ICP.
Here's what that means before you invest more time.

---

## PERSON RESEARCH OUTPUT FORMAT

When person research is requested, run a web search
on the person's name and company and return results
in this exact format:

---

PERSON: [Name], [Title] at [Company]
BACKGROUND: [career history — where they came from,
            how long in current role, relevant prior
            experience]
LINKEDIN ACTIVITY: [recent posts, comments, or
                   engagement if available — what
                   are they publicly talking about?]
RED FLAGS OR OPPORTUNITIES: [recent job change,
                            promotion, company news
                            that affects them
                            personally, shared
                            connections, or anything
                            that creates an opening]

COACHING NOTE:
[One to two sentences on how to approach this person
specifically based on their background. What matters
to them? What is their likely buying lens — strategic,
operational, or financial? Are they a likely champion
or economic buyer based on their role and seniority?]

---

## RULE AFTER RESEARCH COMPLETES

After delivering research output — whether company,
person, or both — the coach must immediately connect
the research to the next coaching action. Never let
research be the endpoint. Always close with:

"Here's what this means for how you go in —
[one sentence connecting research findings to the
rep's next action or approach]."

Then proceed to coaching.

---

## ICP FIT LOGIC

The ICP fit assessment in company research must pull
the user's stored ICP from their seller profile and
compare it against the research findings.

If the user's ICP profile is incomplete or not on
file, do not skip the assessment. Instead note it:

"I don't have your ICP on file yet — want to set
that up so I can assess fit automatically going
forward? Takes 30 seconds."

Then proceed with research output without the
ICP assessment.

---

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

1. Enable the Claude web search tool in the API
   call for both Deal Mode and Coach Mode sessions.

2. The research offer must fire when a company name
   or person's name is introduced — whether at deal
   creation or mid-conversation.

3. Company and person research run as sequential
   web searches in the same session — company first,
   person second — when both are requested.

4. Research output must reference the user's stored
   seller profile (ICP field) for the fit assessment.
   Pull from the database at the time of the search —
   not from session context alone.

5. The research offer should not repeat in the same
   session for the same company or person. If research
   was already run, do not offer it again.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The feature is working correctly when:

1. A user enters a new deal with a company name and
   the coach offers research before coaching begins.

2. A user mentions a person's name in either Deal
   Mode or Coach Mode and the coach offers to research
   them alongside or independently of the company.

3. The user can select company only, person only,
   both, or neither — and the coach responds
   correctly to each answer.

4. Company research output includes an ICP fit
   assessment that references the user's stored
   ICP profile.

5. Person research output includes a coaching note
   that connects the person's background to how
   the rep should approach them.

6. After research is delivered, the coach immediately
   connects findings to the next coaching action
   before proceeding.

7. If the user declines research, the coach proceeds
   directly to coaching without referencing research
   again in that session.
